# Code of Conduct

Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests and discussions, should be able to do so free of harassment, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

**Expected:** be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised content, publishing anyone's private information or voice recordings without permission, and spam.

Report problems to iamqasimrazamalik@gmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, close issues or block contributors who break this code.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
